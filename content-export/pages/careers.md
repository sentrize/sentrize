## Section 5 — Hiring Process (5 steps)

## Our hiring process

1. **Application review** — We read every application and respond within five business days.
2. **Intro conversation** — A 30-minute call to align on the role, your goals, and how we work.
3. **Technical interview** — A practical, real-world problem in your domain — no whiteboard trivia.
4. **Team & systems round** — Meet your future teammates and dig into architecture and trade-offs.
5. **Offer & onboarding** — A clear offer, fast turnaround, and a structured first-30-days plan.
